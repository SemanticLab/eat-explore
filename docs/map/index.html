Where the Engineers Were

The Technical Services Program drew on the research belt around New York: Bell Labs in Murray Hill, IBM, Grumman on Long Island, the universities. Each engineer card carries a typed address. Here those addresses are placed on the map, with the Knowledge Graph's own places and the bibliography's places of publication as further layers.

Addresses are the free text typed on the cards, geocoded with OpenStreetMap Nominatim into a lookup file shipped with this site. Where only the town could be resolved the marker is placed at the town centre and marked approximate. Knowledge Graph and publication places use the coordinates stored in the Wikibase.
