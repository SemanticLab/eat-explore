The Matchmaking File

In 1966–67 E.A.T. kept one card per engineer who volunteered to work with an artist. The typed front gives employer, address and specialities; the handwritten back lists the artists the engineer was proposed to. Pick an engineer to see both sides of the card and the proposed pairings.
